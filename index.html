01 — About

Engineering across the full stack.

I like projects where software has to survive contact with the physical world.

My work spans robotics, control, perception, embedded systems and high-performance computing. I enjoy connecting mathematical models with implementation: designing control logic, integrating sensors and actuators, building ROS pipelines, validating in simulation, and then making the system work on real hardware.

I am especially interested in autonomous robots for challenging environments, reliable mechatronic systems and computational tools that make complex machines more capable.
